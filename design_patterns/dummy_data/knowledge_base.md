# Support knowledge base (demo fixture)

Small, self-contained corpus for the retrieval patterns. Each section is
one retrievable chunk.

## Shipping times
Standard delivery arrives in three to five working days. Express
delivery arrives the next working day when ordered before 6pm.

## Refund policy
Refunds are available for thirty days after delivery. An order cancelled
before delivery is refunded in full automatically.

## Damaged items
Report a damaged item within fourteen days with a photograph. Damaged
items are replaced or refunded at the customer's choice.

## Payment methods
We accept card, bank transfer and store credit. Refunds return to the
original payment method and take three to five working days to appear.

## Order tracking
A tracking reference is issued when an order is dispatched. Tracking
updates once every twenty four hours while in transit.

## Address changes
An address can be changed until the order is dispatched. After dispatch
the parcel must be redirected through the carrier.
