## Findings

Google unveiled its Gemini 4 Argon model on September 30, 2026, after delaying Gemini 3.5 Pro [E1]. The company is providing Argon to select cybersecurity partners and participating in a voluntary pre-release process with the Trump administration, without announcing a public release timeline [E2]. Argon is described as the most performant model for complex workloads, comparable to OpenAI’s Astra and Anthropic’s Opus on coding and cyber benchmarks [E4]. OpenAI released its Astra model, claiming it is the most powerful and capable yet and a new frontier for computer and browser use with unmatched speed, accuracy, and safety [E10][E11]. Google releases Gemini 4 Argon, called its most powerful model yet [E12]

## Details

Cognizant and Cognition deployed autonomous AI engineering at Odyssey Logistics using Cognition’s Devin software engineer, announcing production results and expanded collaborative offerings [E6]. Devin is designed to handle multi-step development tasks—planning, writing, and testing code—working alongside Cognizant engineers [E7]. Training of over 3,000 associates on Devin has cut time to release-ready code by more than 50 % and accelerated greenfield development five to six times [E8]. As a result, delivery throughput rose by roughly a third and Odyssey recorded a 37 % net cost saving versus the conventional approach [E9].
