# Impact of AI on Software Jobs

**Status:** PARTIAL

## Findings

Approximately 79 % of respondents report using AI tools at least once per day, indicating that Generative Artificial Intelligence has become a routine component of professional practice [E1]. Routine coding responsibilities for backend, frontend, and QA engineers are shifting toward higher-level tasks such as architectural reasoning, requirement formulation, and AI system orchestration [E2]. Several respondents predict a polarization effect: junior roles may decline in number, while senior and architect-level competencies become more valuable [E3]. AI delivers the greatest productivity gains in design, implementation, testing, and documentation, with over 70% of developers reporting time halved for boilerplate and documentation tasks [E4].

## Details

A common expectation is a shift from pure code writing toward architectural thinking, requirement formulation, quality assurance, and orchestration of AI systems [E2]. GenAI exerts its highest impact in design, implementation, testing, and documentation, where over 70 % of developers report at least halving the time for boilerplate and documentation tasks [E4]. Several respondents predict a polarization effect: junior roles may decline in number, while senior and architect-level competencies become more valuable [E3].
