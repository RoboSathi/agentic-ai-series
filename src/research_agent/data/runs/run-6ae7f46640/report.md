# Agentic AI Skills for B.Tech CS/AI Students: Evidence-Based Course Design

**Status:** PARTIAL

## Findings

Prompt Engineering & Instruction Design, Planning & Reasoning, Tool use & API Integration, Memory & Knowledge Management are core Agentic AI skills that B.Tech CS/AI students need to learn for future AI-driven software development. [E1][E2][E3][E4] Digital Literacy & AI Fluency and Critical Thinking & Problem-Solving are also core agentic AI skills. [E5][E6] Implementing an AI reading assistant that adapts text difficulty based on student performance improves reading growth metrics by 32%. [E13] Using an AI-powered platform that adapts content presentation to learning preferences increases student engagement by 78%. [E14] Integrating an AI writing assistant that provides targeted feedback on essay structure and reasoning improves students' revision skills and overall writing quality. [E15]

## Details

The course is a hands-on driven program focused on building and deploying production-grade autonomous AI agents rather than just theory. [E16] It consists of 10 weeks of live instructor-led weekend classes (6 hours each) plus self-paced learning modules for additional practice. [E17] Students build 25+ real-world use cases and 5+ industry projects to apply their learning. [E18]
