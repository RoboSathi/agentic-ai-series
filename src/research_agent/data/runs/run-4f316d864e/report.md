# India's Cricket World Cup Finals Journey and Player Participation

**Status:** PARTIAL

## Overview

India has appeared in four Cricket World Cup finals (2003, 2011, 2023) and finished as runners-up on three occasions. The team won the titles in 1983 and 2011.

## Players

Sachin Tendulkar, Virender Sehwag and Harbhajan Singh featured in the 2003 and 2011 ODI World Cup finals [E9][E10][E11]. Each player's progression to the finals is documented with citations [R5].

## Approaches and trends

India's pattern shows early finals appearances (2003) followed by a home-win in 2011, then back-to-back semifinal exits in 2019 and 2023 despite strong group-stage performances. The team remained undefeated en route to the 2023 final before losing to Australia [E8].
