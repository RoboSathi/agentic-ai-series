## Preconditions
- The brief and its rubric have been agreed.

## Procedure
1. Read the rubric first: every sub-question must serve at least one of
   its items.
2. Cover each distinct angle once; merge sub-questions whose searches
   would return the same pages.
3. Write concrete queries that name subjects, not themes.
4. Keep one search in reserve for a follow-up.

## Boundaries
- Do not plan work the rubric does not need.
- Do not pin a query to a year or edition the brief did not name.

## Completion
Stop once every rubric item is served by at least one sub-question.
