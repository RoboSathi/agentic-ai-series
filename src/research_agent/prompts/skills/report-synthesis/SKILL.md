## Preconditions
- The run's evidence has been gathered and verified.

## Procedure
1. Answer the brief's question first, in its own terms.
2. Support each statement with the evidence that states it, cited.
3. Say where the evidence is thin, dated or in conflict.

## Boundaries
- Add nothing the evidence does not contain.
- Do not round, convert or recompute a figure a source gives.

## Completion
Stop once every rubric item is answered or explicitly marked unanswered.
