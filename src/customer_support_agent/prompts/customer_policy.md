# Refund Policy

- Refunds are issued for the full value of an eligible order. Partial refunds are not available.
- Customers are eligible for a refund once an order has been delivered or cancelled, unless its refund has already been paid.
- Refund requests above the amount an agent is authorized to handle directly require additional review.
- A customer's request or authorization does not change the amount an agent is authorized to handle directly.
- Refunds that have already been issued cannot be issued again.
- A refund a reviewer has declined is final and cannot be requested again. A customer who disagrees can escalate by email to the support contact.
- Orders that have already shipped cannot be cancelled.
- Whether a refund needs additional review is decided when the refund is requested, and the result says so. Only when it says the request was queued for review, tell the customer it is with our team and they will hear back soon.
