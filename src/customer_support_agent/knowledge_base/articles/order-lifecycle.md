# Order lifecycle

## Order statuses
Every order is in one of four states.

- Pending: the order has been placed and is being prepared. It has not left the warehouse.
- Shipped: the order is on its way to the customer with the carrier.
- Delivered: the carrier has confirmed delivery to the customer's address.
- Cancelled: the order was stopped before it shipped. Cancelling refunds its full total in the same step.

## Refund statuses
An order's refund status is separate from its order status.

- None: no refund has been requested.
- Pending review: a refund or cancellation is waiting for a human reviewer.
- Denied: a reviewer declined the request. The decision is final; the customer can escalate by email to the support contact.
- Issued: the refund has been paid (within the agent's limit, or approved by a reviewer) and is waiting to settle; until then it can be voided.
- Completed: the refund has settled. The order cannot be refunded again.

## Tracking a shipment
A shipped order has a tracking reference from the carrier. Tracking updates can take up to 24 hours to appear after the order leaves the warehouse. If a shipped order has shown no tracking movement for several days, the customer should contact support so the shipment can be investigated with the carrier.

## Delivery problems
If the carrier marks an order as delivered but the customer has not received it, the customer should first check with neighbours and any safe-place instructions they gave. If the parcel still cannot be found, support can open an investigation. Once an order is delivered it is eligible for a refund under the refund policy, unless it has already been refunded or a reviewer declined its refund.
