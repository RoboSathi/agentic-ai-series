# Refunds

## Which orders can be refunded
A refund applies once an order is settled. An order that has been delivered can be refunded, because the customer has it and wants their money back. An order that was cancelled before delivery can also be refunded, because the customer never received it.

An order that is still pending or shipped is in flight. There is nothing to refund yet, so the customer should wait until it is delivered, or ask for it to be cancelled.

## One refund per order
Each order can be refunded once. When an order already shows a completed refund, it cannot be refunded again. The agent should say so plainly rather than open a second request.

## Full refunds only
A refund is always for the full order total. Partial refunds are not supported, whether the agent issues the refund or a human reviewer approves it.

## Small refunds and human review
Refunds of up to $50 are processed automatically as soon as the order is confirmed as eligible. Refunds above $50 need a human to approve them before any money moves; smaller refunds are never sent for review. These are placed in the review queue, and the customer is told their refund needs a manual review and that they will hear back soon.

A reviewer can approve or deny a queued refund. An approved refund is then issued exactly like an automatic one.

## When the money arrives
Once a refund is issued, it goes back to the original payment method. Card refunds usually show on the statement within 5 to 10 business days, depending on the customer's bank. The customer also receives a confirmation message when the refund is issued.

## What if a refund fails part-way
Issuing a refund updates the order, records the payment and then notifies the customer. If any step fails, the steps already taken are undone, so an order is never marked as refunded without the money being recorded, or the reverse. The customer can simply ask again. Asking twice for the same refund never pays twice.
