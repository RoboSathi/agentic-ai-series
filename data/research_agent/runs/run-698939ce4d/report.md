# Kerala 2026 Public Holidays Overview

**Status:** PARTIAL

## Summary

Kerala's state government published its official 2026 public holiday calendar under Section 25 of the Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881, via the Kerala Gazette Extraordinary on 31 October 2025 [E1]. The calendar designates specific dates for national, religious, and regional observances throughout the year [E2][E3].

## Concepts

The Kerala government has released the official public holiday calendar for 2026, offering residents, government offices, schools, and the banking sector a clear schedule of next year’s notified holidays. Issued under Section 25 of the Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881, the list was published in the Kerala Gazette Extraordinary on 31 October 2025 and confirms the dates when state government institutions and designated establishments will remain closed. [E1] Mahasivarathri – 15 February (Sunday)
Easter – 5 April (Sunday)
Deepavali – 8 November (Sunday) [E4]

## Examples

Mannam Jayanthi – 2 January (Friday)
Republic Day – 26 January (Monday)
Id-ul-Fitr (Ramzan)* – 20 March (Friday) [E2] Major festival holidays comprise Vishu on 15 April, May Day on 1 May, Id-ul-Adha on 27 May, Independence Day on 15 August, First Onam/Milad-i-Sherif on 25 August, and Thiruvonam on 26 August [E3].
