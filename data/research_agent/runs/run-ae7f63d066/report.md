# Vedam College BTech CS/AI Program Overview

**Status:** PARTIAL

## Findings

The program offers a 4-year B.Tech in Computer Science with an AI specialization awarded by Ajeenkya DY Patil University [E1]. Vedam sits on a lush, 100-acre campus at Ajeenkya DY Patil University, Lohegaon — just 6 kilometres from Pune International Airport [E2]. Students receive a personal MacBook as part of the course fee [E3][E7].

## Details

The AI-First Curriculum is designed to equip students with the skills, tools, and mindset needed to thrive in an AI-powered world [E4]. From Day 1, students write real code, build projects, and solve industry-relevant problems [E5]. Students learn from top instructors who are engineers, researchers, and industry leaders from Google, Microsoft, Amazon, and leading startups [E6]. Students in the program receive a 6-month paid internship and preparation for global competitions such as GSOC, ICPC, and Imagine Cup [E9]. The program provides up to ₹1 Crore seed fund for student startups [E10]. A video features real student reviews that discuss the pros and cons of Vedam School of Technology's BTech CS/AI program [E8].
