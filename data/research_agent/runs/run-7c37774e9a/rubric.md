# Rubric

- R1 [must] Every option is assessed against every stated criterion. (deterministic:subject_criterion_coverage ≥ 1)
- R2 [must] Every factual sentence in the report cites collected evidence. (deterministic:citations_per_claim ≥ 1)
- R3 [must] Cited claims are supported by their quoted spans. (nli:claims_entailed ≥ 1)
- R4 [soft] Where sources disagree, the counter-evidence is reported. (deterministic:counter_evidence_reported ≥ 1)
- R5 [soft] The recommendation states its trade-offs fairly. (judge:tradeoffs_stated ≥ 0.7)
- R6 [must] Evidence on 'Quality of the college' is cited for each option. (deterministic:criterion_evidence ≥ 1)
- R7 [must] Evidence on 'working conditions' is cited for each option. (deterministic:criterion_evidence ≥ 1)
- R8 [must] Evidence on 'career growth' is cited for each option. (deterministic:criterion_evidence ≥ 1)
- R9 [must] Evidence on 'compensation' is cited for each option. (deterministic:criterion_evidence ≥ 1)
- R10 [must] Evidence on 'academic environment' is cited for each option. (deterministic:criterion_evidence ≥ 1)
- R11 [must] Every cited link was fetched and resolved. (deterministic:links_resolved ≥ 1)
- R12 [must] The report establishes at least 1 cited finding(s). (deterministic:findings_present ≥ 1)
